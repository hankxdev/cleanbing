# Privacy Policy — Clean Bing

_Last updated: 2026-08-30_

Clean Bing is a browser extension that hides clutter on `bing.com`. Your
privacy is simple to describe because the extension is built to need nothing
from you.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Clean Bing does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any
personal or usage data.

Specifically, the extension does **not**:

- Collect personally identifiable information (name, email, address, etc.)
- Collect health, financial, authentication, or location information
- Read, record, or transmit your searches, browsing history, or page content
- Use cookies, analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers
- Create an account or require a sign-in
- Send any data to the developer or to any third-party server

## How it works

Clean Bing runs entirely on your own device, only on pages under
`*://*.bing.com/*`. It applies CSS rules and a small content script that hide
elements (the MSN feed, Rewards banners, trending widgets) and reset the search
box placeholder to "Search". All of this happens locally in your browser. No
network requests are made by the extension, and no information ever leaves your
computer.

## Permissions

The extension requests only a content script match for `*://*.bing.com/*`,
which lets it modify the appearance of Bing pages you visit. It requests no
other permissions.

## Data sharing

Because no data is collected, no data is shared with anyone.
